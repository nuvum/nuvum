# Greeting credits

The translations come from the [English Wiktionary entry for “hello”](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/hello#Translations), written by [Wiktionary contributors](https://en.wiktionary.org/w/index.php?title=hello&action=history) and extracted by [Kaikki/Wiktextract](https://kaikki.org/dictionary/English/meaning/h/he/hello.jsonl). Short forms were selected, deduplicated, and ordered for the animation. The greeting data is available under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
